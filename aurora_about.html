About Satoshi Lab

Web3 engineering + growth studio built for launch

Who we are

Satoshi Lab is a Web3 engineering and growth studio headquartered in Dubai. We build smart contracts, Web3 products, and the community + PR infrastructure that makes crypto launches count. We're not a consultancy that disappears after handoff — we stay accountable from whitepaper to mainnet.

We're part of Braindeck Infotech Private Limited, a diversified technology holding company incorporated in Delhi (CIN: U72100DL2010PTC206927). Beyond Satoshi Lab, we operate eight business verticals spanning Web3, education, fintech, real estate, and technology.

Our edge

One team. Full accountability. Unlike most crypto agencies, we don't hand over code and disappear. We manage the entire launch — engineering, product, security, community, and PR — under one roof. No vendor chaos. No fingerpointing.

We ship. We've launched tokens, built dApps that process real volume, and grown communities from zero. Our work is live on Ethereum, Polygon, BNB Chain, and Base.

We're honest. No fake metrics. No guaranteed moon missions. We deliver defined work — code, placements, community size — and let the market do what it does.

We educate. Through SatoshiLab Academy, we've trained hundreds to understand and build in Web3. We believe crypto adoption requires demystifying crypto.
