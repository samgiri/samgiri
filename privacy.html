Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This policy explains how Satoshi Lab, operated by Braindeck Infotech Private Limited ("Satoshi Lab", "we", "us"), handles information submitted through this website.

Information you provide

When you contact us, enquire about a service, or express interest in an Academy course, we may receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, email address, message, and course or service interest.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, discuss requested services or learning opportunities, process Academy enrollments where applicable, and operate and improve this website. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected. As a general practice, enquiry and contact-form submissions are retained for up to 24 months from your last interaction with us, unless a longer period is required to comply with legal, accounting, or regulatory obligations, or to resolve disputes. Academy enrollment records, once courses are live, will be retained for the duration of enrollment plus any period required for certificate verification and applicable tax/accounting requirements.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Essential technical cookies or local storage may be used to enable core site functionality. Our hosting and infrastructure providers may collect standard server logs (such as IP address and request metadata) for security and performance purposes. If we add analytics tools in the future, we will update this policy accordingly.

Service providers and international processing

We use the following third-party service providers to operate this website. Each is governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Formspree (contact and enquiry forms) — a US-based service provider.
  • Vercel (website hosting) — a US-based service provider.
  • Zoho Mail (email) — data hosted in Zoho's India data center.

Where information is processed outside your home country, we rely on the safeguards each provider offers under their own terms and applicable law.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing us using the contact details below. We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.

Children's privacy

This website and our services are not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
